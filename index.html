02 / OUR APPROACH

A RESPECTFUL POINT OF VIEW

Observe the form.
Respect the subject.

Figure study is a language of line, proportion, posture and expression. This archive treats the human body as a subject of artistic observation—not as an invitation to objectify.

“The gesture is brief.
The study asks us to look closer.”THE STUDIO NOTEBOOK
